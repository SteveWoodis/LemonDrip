Catering Margins Are Thin and Easy to Lose Track Of

A catering event looks like a win on paper — the invoice was paid, Square processed the final balance, and the client was thrilled. But the prep hours were longer than expected, you brought an extra staff member, and the rental van cost more than budgeted. Now what was your actual margin?

Catering is a high-cost-per-event business. Food, labor, transport, equipment rentals, permits, and service fees stack up before you plate a single dish. Most caterers track revenue well. Few track the costs with the same discipline — until something goes wrong.

VenView gives you per-event profit visibility so you can quote confidently, staff appropriately, and grow without leaking margin on every job.
