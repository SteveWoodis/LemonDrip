Square Gives You Sales. It Doesn't Give You Profit.

Square is an excellent POS. It processes payments, tracks transactions, and generates sales summaries. But Square reports are built for sales history — not event profitability. They don't know your booth fees, your staffing costs, your supply spend, or your permit expenses. They show gross sales and fees. Profit is your problem.

Most event vendors manually export Square reports and try to reconcile them in spreadsheets — an error-prone, time-consuming process that still doesn't surface clean per-event margin.

VenView fills the gap. It connects directly to Square, pulls the right data for each event, and combines it with your real operating costs to produce a true profit number. No spreadsheets. No exports. No manual math.

How the Square + VenView Integration Works

1) Connect Once

Authorize VenView to access your Square account through a secure OAuth connection. Your credentials never touch VenView's servers — Square handles the authentication.

2) Sync Per Event

When you create an event in VenView, pull your Square sales data for that event's date and location. Gross sales, refunds, discounts, tips, and Square fees all imported automatically.

3) Add Costs, Get Profit

Layer in your real event costs — booth fees, labor, supplies, permits — and VenView calculates true net profit and margin instantly.

What VenView Pulls from Square — and What It Adds

What Comes from Square

Gross sales, net sales, itemized refunds, discount amounts, tip totals, gift card sales, cash vs. card breakdowns, and Square processing fees — all pulled automatically for the event period.

What VenView Adds on Top

Booth and event fees, labor and staff costs, supply and ingredient costs, health department permits, mileage reimbursement, and any custom event expenses — the costs Square will never know about.

The Result: True Event Profit

Net profit, profit margin, and a full cost-by-category breakdown — all in one place, for every event you run. Stored permanently so you can compare performance over time.

Multi-Location Support

If you run multiple Square locations — different markets, different trucks, different setups — VenView handles them all. Each event maps to the right Square location automatically.
